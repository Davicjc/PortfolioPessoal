About

Website for Audicom Tecnologia, a Secullum, Topdata and Control iD reseller in Uberlândia: access control, time clocks, turnstiles and facial recognition. It has a dynamic product catalog filtered by category and a Firebase-backed admin panel for the team to manage products and content, all in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Outcome

The company got its own product showcase, updated by the team without touching code.
